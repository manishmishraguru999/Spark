DEEP RESEARCH BRIEF — AI INFLUENCER LAUNCH (INDIA, 16–30, MULTI-LANGUAGE)

## ROLE
Act as a research team of five specialists working together:
1. Creator-economy analyst (YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels growth)
2. AI production engineer (local GPU + cloud generative video/voice)
3. Platform-policy and Indian tech-law researcher
4. Monetization strategist (Indian market, INR)
5. Trust & safety auditor (finds unethical growth tactics and their ethical replacements)

## MISSION
Validate, stress-test and improve the launch plan below. Find what is true, what is outdated,
what is risky, and what is missing. Produce a final, evidence-backed, executable plan.
Use deep, multi-step web research. Do not answer from memory where a current source exists.

## CONTEXT — WHAT IS ALREADY KNOWN (verify, do not assume)
Project: An AI-generated influencer persona (male, female or stylized; to be tested).
NOT a tutorial channel. NOT kids content.
- Audience: 16–30 years, India first, then global.
- Languages: Hinglish first; Hindi, English and other Indian languages later.
- Platforms: YouTube Shorts (primary), Instagram Reels (secondary), YouTube long-form later.
- Format idea: one consistent AI character, 15–40s Shorts, original opinions, reactions,
  trend commentary, daily-life storytelling. Hook in first 2 seconds. 1 Short per day.
- Creator: solo, senior VFX compositor (Nuke, After Effects, Blender, DaVinci Resolve,
  ComfyUI, Unity/UE5).
- Hardware: RTX 3060 12GB VRAM, i9-12900K, 64GB RAM, Windows.
- Budget: ₹5,000 per month maximum. No loans or borrowed money.
- Stated goal: 1,00,000 subscribers in 30 days. Earlier research flagged this as very
  unlikely. Re-verify with data.

Earlier findings (to re-check; labels show confidence at the time):

  Median monthly growth at 100K–1M subs ≈ 0.30%; at 10K–100K ≈ 0.97% (TubeAI, Sep 2026).

  targets mass-produced, template, near-identical content. AI as a tool stays eligible.

- [REPORTED] Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B runs on 12GB VRAM via ComfyUI offloading; ~several minutes
  per 5s clip at 480p, 10–15 min at higher res. LTX-Video unstable on 12GB + low RAM.

  Hailuo ~$9.99–20/mo, Veo ~$4.99/mo or API ~$0.05/sec, ElevenLabs Starter ~₹420/mo
  (INR billing + UPI officially supported).
- [VERIFIED via vidIQ, IN, Oct 2026] Big Indian search demand: "free ai video generator"
  ~4.3 lakh/month in-country; "ai tools" ~2.25 lakh in-country (competition 66);
  "ai side hustle" competition 73 and falling (-26%).

## NON-NEGOTIABLE RESEARCH RULES
1. Evidence label on every claim: VERIFIED (primary/official source or reproducible data)
   · REPORTED (credible secondary source) · INFERENCE (your reasoning, show logic)
   · UNKNOWN (not found) · CONFLICT (sources disagree, show both) · OUTDATED (older than
   12 months for fast-moving topics).
2. Source hierarchy: official platform help/policy pages, government gazettes and court
   orders, company pricing pages > peer-reviewed or large-sample studies > reputable
   news > creator blogs and aggregators. Prefer the higher tier when sources conflict.
3. Cite every factual claim with a URL and the date you accessed it. No URL = mark UNKNOWN.
4. Never invent numbers, case studies, channel names, prices or legal text. If you cannot
   find it, say so.
5. Prices: give the official price, the date, and the INR equivalent with the exchange
   rate and date used. Note GST and first-period discounts.
6. Separate global data from India data. Separate YouTube from Instagram.
7. For case studies, include only accounts whose numbers are publicly verifiable
   (Social Blade, platform pages, credible press). Mark unverifiable stories as REPORTED.
8. Kids content, child-directed channels and Made-for-Kids strategy are OUT OF SCOPE.
9. Unethical tactics may be described only at awareness level (what it is, how common,
   how platforms detect it, consequences). Do NOT give step-by-step instructions for
   carrying them out. Always pair each with an ethical alternative.

## RESEARCH MODULES (complete all)

### M1 — Goal reality check
- Find the best available data on how fast new channels (especially AI-persona and Shorts
  channels) reach 10K, 100K and 1M subscribers.
- Find 5–10 verifiable fast-growth cases (2024–2026), AI-persona or faceless preferred.
  For each: niche, format, posting frequency, time to 100K, what triggered growth,
  and whether the channel is still active and monetized.
- Output: probability-style assessment of "100K in 30 days" (INFERENCE, show reasoning),
  plus realistic conservative / base / stretch targets for Day 30, 90 and 180.

### M2 — Persona and niche validation (16–30, India, multi-language)
- Compare 6–8 persona/content concepts suited to an AI character for this audience
  (e.g. trend commentary, relatable daily-life comedy, motivation/self-improvement,
  tech-and-AI news, pop-culture reactions, fictional story series, fashion/lifestyle
  virtual model, music/vibe content).
- For each: demand signals (search + Shorts trends), competition, India vs global
  reach, multi-language potential, AI production difficulty, policy risk, brand-deal
  potential, long-term durability (not just a 30-day spike).
- Score each 1–10 on these criteria and rank. Recommend top 2 with evidence.
- Check whether audiences engage with AI personas or reject them: find studies or
  surveys on trust and engagement with virtual influencers (India data if available).

### M3 — Platform mechanics (official sources first)
- YouTube Shorts: what YouTube officially says about recommendations, retention,
  "viewed vs swiped away", Shorts monetization, YPP thresholds as of today, and any
  announced threshold changes (verify the reported Feb 2027 change).
- Instagram Reels: Meta's official guidance on Reels ranking, originality, watermark
  and repost penalties, AI labels ("AI info"), Reels monetization in India.
- Hook, length, posting-time and frequency findings with sources (not guru opinion).
- Cross-posting: risks and correct way (no watermarks, native upload, per-platform edits).

### M4 — AI production stack: local vs cloud
For each pipeline step (script, voice, character image, consistent face, lip-sync /
talking head, motion video, music/SFX, edit, subtitles, multi-language dubbing):
- Best free/local option that runs on RTX 3060 12GB + 64GB RAM (with real benchmark
  sources), setup difficulty, time per 30s Short.
- Best low-cost cloud option, official price, INR cost per finished 30s Short.
- Character consistency methods (LoRA training, IP-Adapter, reference images) and
  which work on 12GB.
- Lip-sync / talking-avatar options (local and cloud) and their quality limits for Hindi.
- Multi-language dubbing options (including YouTube's own auto-dubbing) and quality.
- Output: a comparison table, then one recommended hybrid pipeline that produces
  1 Short per day within ₹5,000/month and about 3 hours/day of work.

### M5 — Policy, legal and compliance (India + platforms)
- YouTube: inauthentic content policy text, AI/altered-content disclosure rules,
  impersonation and misleading-metadata rules, spam/deceptive-practices policy.
- Meta/Instagram: AI labeling, branded content rules, inauthentic behavior policy.
- India: IT Rules 2021 and any amendments or advisories on synthetic/deepfake content
  and labeling (check MeitY notifications 2023–2026, current status); DPDP Act 2023
  obligations for creators collecting data; ASCI influencer-advertising guidelines
  (disclosure of paid deals, virtual influencer rules); Consumer Protection rules on
  misleading endorsements.
- Personality and publicity rights: Indian court orders protecting celebrities against
  AI voice/face misuse (find actual cases and dates). What this means for an AI persona.
- Copyright: AI-generated music/visual ownership, licensing terms of each tool used
  (commercial use allowed or not), music on Shorts/Reels.
- Output: compliance checklist with source per item.

### M6 — UNETHICAL TACTICS AUDIT (trust & safety specialist)
Research the black-hat and grey-hat growth tactics commonly used by AI and faceless
creators. For EACH tactic below (add more if you find them), give:
| Tactic | What it is (1 line) | How common (evidence) | How platforms detect it |
| Real consequences (bans, demonetization, legal cases, with sources) |
| Why it fails long-term | Ethical alternative that targets the same goal |

Tactics to investigate:
1. Buying subscribers, followers, views, likes, comments (bot/farm services)
2. Sub-for-sub, engagement pods, comment-exchange groups
3. Re-uploading or lightly editing other creators' videos ("reused content")
4. AI deepfakes of celebrities or real people (face or voice) for views
5. Not disclosing that the persona is AI; pretending the AI character is a real human
6. Fake giveaways, fake urgency, misleading thumbnails and titles (clickbait lies)
7. Mass-produced template Shorts at scale from one script
8. Rage-bait, fake news, fabricated "leaks" or emotionally manipulative stories
9. Undisclosed paid promotions, fake reviews, affiliate links without disclosure
10. Scraping trending audio/music without licence; copyright-strike evasion tricks
11. Multiple duplicate channels to game the algorithm; channel buying/selling
12. Misleading "earn money" or finance claims by an AI persona
13. Sexualized or suggestive AI content used as clickbait for a 16–30 audience
14. Hashtag stuffing, keyword stuffing, misleading metadata
Rules: awareness-level only, no execution steps. Every row must end with the ethical,
policy-safe way to reach the same outcome.

### M7 — Monetization (first 90 days, India)
- When YPP and Instagram monetization become realistic given M1 targets.
- Non-ad income for an AI persona: brand deals (find Indian rate benchmarks per 1K
  followers with sources), affiliate programs available in India, digital products,
  licensing the character, UGC-style ad work.
- Which brands work with virtual influencers in India (verifiable examples).
- Output: a 90-day monetization roadmap with milestones and realistic INR ranges

### M8 — Budget model

- What to cut first if costs rise. What to upgrade first if revenue starts.

### M9 — 30-day execution plan

- Weekly review checklist (metrics to read in YouTube Studio and Instagram Insights,
  and the decision each metric triggers).
- A/B test plan: 2 personas × 3 formats in weeks 1–2, then double down.

### M10 — Risk register
Top 15 risks (policy, legal, technical, financial, reputational, burnout), each with
likelihood, impact, early warning sign, mitigation, and source where applicable.

### M11 — Viral niches and big-creator growth tactics
A. Viral-with-few-videos channels:
- Find 20+ channels (2025–2026) that went viral with fewer than 30 videos, Shorts first.
  Use outlier/breakout data (vidIQ Outliers, Nexlev, Social Blade, Playboard, viewstats).
  For each: niche, format, first-upload date, uploads, subs, top video views, hook,
  title pattern, thumbnail/first-frame style, and whether AI-made.
- Starting leads to verify (from Nexlev, Oct 2026): pet-psychology explainers
  (ZawPolyShorts, DiloPolyShorts), recurring AI character (猫のミミ / Mimi),
  numbered series ("Earth 6030 future"), transformations (X Edit, Aura Frame),
  ranking formats (HeroRanking), Indian AI miniature cooking ASMR (Miniature Dollverse,
  Nazin Tiny Food, Create Clay).
- Separate durable niches from one-hit spikes. Note survivorship bias.
- Which of these patterns transfer to a Hinglish AI persona for 16–30 in India?
B. Big-creator tactics:
- What top creators (MrBeast, Indian creators like BeerBiceps, Dhruv Rathee, CarryMinati,
  Mythpat, Technical Guruji, Nikhil Sharma) are documented to do on packaging, hooks,

  creator podcasts, official YouTube Creator Insider/Liaison statements, and the

- Shorts-specific: viewed vs swiped, loops, first-frame motion, captions, length,

- Output: "Tactic → evidence → how a solo AI-persona creator applies it ethically".

## HOW TO USE YOUR FULL CAPABILITIES
- Browse widely and deeply: at least 40 distinct sources, prioritizing official ones.
  Re-search when the first results are thin, old or contradictory.
- Use multi-step reasoning: plan sub-questions first, research each, then synthesize.
- Use code execution / data analysis (if available) for: growth projections, cost per
  Short, INR conversions, scoring matrices. Show formulas or code.
- Create tables and, if supported, charts (growth scenarios, cost breakdown).
- If you can read video pages or transcripts, inspect 3–5 top AI-persona Shorts in this
  space and note hook structure, length, cuts per second, captions style.
- Cross-check each key number with at least two independent sources. If only one
  exists, label it REPORTED (single source).
- At the end, run a self-verification pass: list your 10 most important claims, re-check
  each source, and downgrade any claim that does not hold up.

## OUTPUT FORMAT

names, policy names, table headers and code in English. Professional tone, no filler.

Structure:
1. Executive summary (10 bullet points max, with labels)
2. Verdict on the "100K in 30 days" goal + realistic targets
3. Persona & niche ranking (table + top 2 recommendation)
4. Platform mechanics summary (YouTube vs Instagram table)
5. Production stack comparison + recommended hybrid pipeline
6. Compliance checklist (India + platforms)
7. Unethical tactics audit table (M6)

11. Risk register
12. What changed vs the earlier plan (corrections, outdated items)
13. Open questions that need the creator's decision
14. Full source list (URL + access date + tier)
15. Confidence score per section (High / Medium / Low) with reason

### C1. ChatGPT — Deep Research (Master Niche Analyst)

```text
ROLE: You are a senior creator-economy research analyst. Run a deep, multi-step web
research project. Use code/data analysis for every calculation.

PROJECT: Choose the best niche for a NEW AI-generated influencer persona (male, female
or stylized character) launching on YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.
Audience: 16–30, India first, then global. Languages: Hinglish/Hindi first, English and
other Indian languages later. Solo creator, budget ₹5,000/month.
OUT OF SCOPE: kids/children's content, production/how-to-make details (research only).

CANDIDATE NICHES (from vidIQ + Nexlev data, Oct 2026 — verify, do not assume):
1. "What-if / imagination" Hindi series (e.g. "अगर डायनासोरों की दुनिया में एक बांस गिर जाये तो"
   — 14.9K-sub channel, 1.13 crore views; "history in minutes" searches +8,956%)
2. Ranking / tier-list format (several new ranking Shorts channels reached 40K–232K subs
   within 60 days with 9–34 uploads)
3. Engineering wonders / unusual things + facts (13K-sub channel, 1.05 crore views)
4. Tech & AI future-news reaction hosted by an AI persona (rising: OpenAI DevDay,
   Meta Connect 2026, Tesla Roadster 2026, flying cars, AI video generators)
5. Desi relatable family/friendship story series with recurring AI characters
   (3.9K-sub channel, 92 lakh views; #aistory Hindi content trending)
6. Pet-character POV stories (Hindi dog story 29 lakh views; pet-psychology channel
   118K subs with 10 videos)
You may add up to 3 better niches if evidence supports them.

RESEARCH TASKS:
T1. For each niche: demand (search + Shorts views), growth trend over 3, 6, 12 months,
    competition (number and size of channels in Hindi and English), India vs global.
T2. Find 5 verifiable example channels per niche (launched 2025–2026), with subs, uploads,
    top video views, first upload date, and whether AI-made. Verify on Social Blade or
    the channel page. Do not invent channels.
T3. Durability test: is the niche a 30-day spike or a 2+ year evergreen? Show evidence.
T4. Monetization potential per niche in India (Shorts RPM ranges, brand-deal fit,
    affiliate fit) with sources. Label estimates.
T5. Policy risk per niche (YouTube inauthentic content, AI disclosure, emotionally
    manipulative content, copyright, likeness rights).
T6. Score each niche 1–10 on: Demand, Growth, Competition gap, Durability,
    Monetization, AI-persona fit, Multi-language fit, Policy safety. Weighted total
    (Durability and Policy safety weight ×1.5). Show the code/table.
T7. Recommend Top 2 niches + 1 backup, with reasons and a "kill criteria"
    (what data in 14 days would prove the niche wrong).

RULES:
- Label every claim: VERIFIED / REPORTED / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN / CONFLICT.
- Cite URL + access date for every fact. Official and primary sources first.
- Separate India data from global data. Separate YouTube from Instagram.
- No invented numbers, channels or quotes. Say UNKNOWN when not found.
- Use at least 30 sources.

OUTPUT (Hindi in Devanagari mixed with English technical terms; tables in English headers):
1. Executive summary (8 bullets)  2. Niche scorecard table  3. Per-niche deep dive
4. Example channels table  5. Top 2 + backup recommendation  6. Kill criteria
7. Open questions  8. Source list with dates  9. Confidence per section
Also export the scorecard as CSV.
```

### C2. Gemini — Deep Research (YouTube + Google Trends Data Specialist)

```text
ROLE: You are a YouTube data specialist. Use Google Search grounding, Google Trends,
and YouTube public data as primary sources. Run Deep Research.

CONTEXT: I am researching niches for a new AI influencer persona on YouTube Shorts.
Audience 16–30, India first (Hindi/Hinglish), later English and other Indian languages.
Research only — no production advice. Kids content is out of scope.

NICHES TO ANALYZE:
1. What-if / imagination series (Hindi)   2. Ranking / tier-list Shorts
3. Engineering wonders & unusual-things facts   4. Tech & AI future-news reaction
5. Desi relatable family/friendship story series   6. Pet-character POV stories

TASKS:
G1. Google Trends (India + Worldwide, past 12 months and past 5 years) for 3–5 core
    search terms per niche in Hindi and English (e.g. "what if", "अगर ऐसा हो जाए तो",
    "ranking", "amazing facts in hindi", "AI news", "hindi kahani", "dog story").
    Report direction (rising / stable / falling), seasonality, and top Indian states.
G2. YouTube search landscape: for each niche, list the top 10 Hindi Shorts channels and
    top 10 English Shorts channels by recent views. Note subs, upload frequency,
    average views of last 10 Shorts, and channel age. Identify gaps (languages or
    sub-topics with demand but few strong channels).
G3. Format analysis: from the top 20 Shorts per niche, extract title length, hook type
    in first 2 seconds, video length, use of captions, recurring characters or series,
    and posting time if visible.
G4. YouTube official sources only: what YouTube (Help Center, Creator Insider,
    YouTube Liaison, official blog) says in 2025–2026 about Shorts recommendations,
    "viewed vs swiped away", AI-generated content labels, and inauthentic content.
G5. Language opportunity: compare Hindi vs Tamil vs Telugu vs Bengali vs Marathi
    demand for the top 2 niches. Where is competition lowest relative to demand?

RULES: Evidence labels on every claim (VERIFIED / REPORTED / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN).
Cite every source with URL and date. Never invent channel names or numbers.

OUTPUT: Hindi (Devanagari) mixed with English technical terms.
1. Trends summary table per niche  2. Channel landscape tables  3. Format pattern table
4. Official YouTube policy summary  5. Language opportunity matrix
6. Top 2 niches by data, with reasons  7. Sources
Export tables to Google Sheets and the report to Google Docs.
```

### C3. Perplexity — Pro / Research mode (Fact-Checker: Policy, Legal, Money)

```text
ROLE: You are a fact-checking researcher. Every sentence must have a citation.
Prefer official domains: support.google.com, blog.youtube, youtube.com/creators,
about.meta.com, transparency.meta.com, help.instagram.com, meity.gov.in,
egazette.gov.in, ascionline.in, indiankanoon.org, consumeraffairs.nic.in.

CONTEXT: Research for a new AI-generated influencer persona on YouTube Shorts and
Instagram Reels, audience 16–30 in India, Hinglish first. Research only. Kids content
out of scope. Candidate niches: what-if/imagination series, ranking/tier lists,
engineering wonders & facts, tech & AI news reaction, desi family/friendship story
series with AI characters, pet-character POV stories.

VERIFY AND REPORT:
P1. YouTube Partner Program: current eligibility thresholds for Shorts and long-form,
    Shorts revenue sharing, and any announced 2026–2027 changes. Is the reported change
    effective 1 Feb 2027 real? Quote the official text.
P2. YouTube "inauthentic content" policy: exact wording, examples, effective dates.
    How it applies to AI-generated personas, AI voices and AI story channels.
P3. YouTube altered/synthetic content disclosure rules: when disclosure is mandatory.
P4. Meta/Instagram: AI labeling ("AI info"), originality rules for Reels, monetization
    options available in India today.
P5. India law: IT Rules 2021 and any 2025–2026 amendments on synthetically generated
    content and labeling (status: draft or notified?), DPDP Act 2023 duties relevant to
    creators, ASCI influencer guidelines including virtual influencers, Consumer
    Protection endorsement rules.
P6. Personality rights: list Indian court orders (2023–2026) protecting celebrities
    against AI misuse of face or voice. Case names, courts, dates.
P7. Copyright: who owns AI-generated video/voice in India; music rules on Shorts/Reels.
P8. For each of the 6 niches: list the specific policy risks and the compliant approach.

RULES: Label VERIFIED (official/primary) / REPORTED (secondary) / CONFLICT / UNKNOWN.
If an official source is not found, say so. Do not summarize from blogs as if official.

OUTPUT (Hindi Devanagari mixed with English terms):
1. Compliance checklist table (item, rule, source, status)  2. Per-niche risk table
3. YPP and monetization facts  4. India legal summary with case list
5. Items still unverified  6. Full citation list
```

### C5. Claude — Research mode (Strategist, Critic, Ethics Auditor)

```text
ROLE: You are a skeptical senior strategist and trust-and-safety auditor. Use web
research and extended thinking. Your job is to stress-test the niche choice, not to
cheerlead.

CONTEXT: New AI-generated influencer persona for YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels.
Audience 16–30, India first, Hinglish, later multi-language. Solo creator with VFX
skills, ₹5,000/month budget, RTX 3060 12GB local PC. The creator wanted 1 lakh
subscribers in 30 days; earlier research found this very unlikely (only ~1.3% of
channels ever reach 100K, per vidIQ 2026). Research only. Kids content out of scope.

CANDIDATE NICHES (with early evidence from vidIQ/Nexlev, Oct 2026):
1. What-if/imagination Hindi series  2. Ranking/tier lists  3. Engineering wonders & facts
4. Tech & AI news reaction  5. Desi family/friendship story series with AI characters
6. Pet-character POV stories

TASKS:
C1. Red-team each niche: the 3 strongest reasons it could fail for an AI persona.
    Include saturation, audience fatigue with AI content, policy changes, and
    survivorship bias in the viral examples.
C2. Unethical-tactics audit for these niches (awareness level only, no how-to):
    fake facts presented as real, AI visuals passed off as real footage, emotional
    manipulation in stories, clickbait that the video does not deliver, reused content,
    celebrity likeness misuse, undisclosed AI persona, bought engagement. For each:
    how common, platform detection, consequences (with sources), and the ethical
    alternative that reaches the same goal.
C3. Persona-niche fit: what personality, voice and values should the AI persona have
    for each top niche so viewers trust it? What makes AI personas feel authentic
    (research on virtual influencers, parasocial trust)?
C4. Realistic growth model: conservative / base / stretch subscriber and view targets
    for Day 30, 90, 180 per top niche, with reasoning and sources.
C5. Final decision memo: pick Top 2 niches and explain trade-offs in plain language.

RULES: Evidence labels on every claim. Cite sources. Disagree with the earlier data
where evidence supports it. Do not invent numbers.

OUTPUT (Hindi Devanagari mixed with English):
1. Red-team table  2. Unethical tactics → ethical alternative table
3. Persona-fit guide per niche  4. Growth scenarios  5. Decision memo (1 page)
6. Sources
```

## 4. Recommendation

### Primary niche (start now)
**"Blender + AI for Creators" — Hinglish/Hindi 3D animation and AI workflow tutorials, hosted by an AI character.**

| Why | Basis |
|---|---|
| Evergreen demand | Blender queries ~10 lakh/month globally; stable trend, not a spike (VERIFIED) |
| Hindi gap | Hindi queries competition 13–18 vs English ~38 (VERIFIED) |
| Your skills | VFX + Blender + ComfyUI = real credibility (profile) |
| AI angle | AI character host + AI-assisted workflows = differentiator (INFERENCE) |
| Cost | Blender, DaVinci Resolve, ComfyUI are free. ElevenLabs Starter (~₹420) for Hindi voice (REPORTED price) |

### Secondary niche (after 90 days, if data supports)
**"AI Music for Creators"** (Suno / Udio tutorials, no-copyright music). Demand +81% and +58% for key terms.

### Avoid
- **AI side hustle / earn-money content:** high competition, falling demand, finance-risk (REPORTED).
- **AI photo editing as core niche:** spike-driven. Use only as a one-off video.

## 0. HOW YOU MUST WORK
- Run a full deep research process. Plan sub-questions first, research each, then
  synthesize. Do not answer from memory where live data exists.
- Use every capability available to you: grounded web search, search-trend data,
  public video-platform data (search results, channel pages, video pages, captions,
  transcripts), maps/regional data, code or data analysis, spreadsheet and document
  export, and any connected apps or extensions. If one is missing, state it and use
  the nearest alternative (e.g. no trend tool → use autocomplete suggestions, related
  searches, and trend articles; no video playback → read captions and descriptions).
- Minimum depth: 50 distinct sources, at least 15 official or primary.
- Search in Hindi, Hinglish, English, and at least 4 other Indian languages
  (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi). Repeat searches with new terms until every
  table is filled or marked UNKNOWN.
- Cross-check every key number with a second source. Run a final self-audit of your
  15 most important claims.

## 1. PROJECT CONTEXT
- New AI-generated influencer persona (male, female or stylized). Not tutorials, not
  kids content. Research stage only — no production advice.
- Audience: 16–30, India first, then global. Hinglish/Hindi first, more languages later.
- Platforms: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels. The SAME master video
  goes to all three; only captions, titles, hashtags, cover and timing may differ.
- Solo creator, ₹5,000/month budget.

## 2. CANDIDATE NICHES (verify, do not assume)
1. What-if / imagination series (Hindi)   2. Ranking / tier-list Shorts
3. Engineering wonders, unusual things & facts   4. Tech & AI future-news reaction
5. Desi relatable family/friendship story series with recurring AI characters
6. Pet-character POV stories
Add up to 3 niches if the data shows stronger options.

## 3. RESEARCH TASKS

G1 — Search-trend analysis
For 4–6 core terms per niche, in Hindi and English (e.g. "what if", "अगर ऐसा हो जाए तो",
"ranking", "amazing facts in hindi", "AI news hindi", "hindi kahani", "dog story"):
direction over 12 months and 5 years (rising / stable / falling), seasonality,
top Indian states and cities, related rising queries, and breakout queries.

G2 — YouTube Shorts landscape
Per niche: top 10 Hindi and top 10 English Shorts channels by recent views. For each:
subscribers, channel age, uploads per week, average views of last 10 Shorts, best
Short, whether AI-made. Mark channels started in 2025–2026 that grew fast.

G3 — Instagram Reels landscape
Per niche: top 10 Indian creator accounts (Hindi/Hinglish) and top 5 global. Followers,
average Reel views (where visible), posting frequency, AI or human, cross-posting to
YouTube and Facebook.

G4 — Facebook Reels landscape
Per niche: top Indian pages/creators posting Reels, followers, typical views, whether
the same videos appear on YouTube/Instagram. Note how Facebook audience age differs
from the other two platforms for 16–30 viewers in India.

G5 — Format pattern extraction
From the top 20 short videos per niche (all platforms combined): title length, first
2-second hook type, total length, captions style, recurring character or series,
loop ending, music/voice style, text on screen, and visible posting time. Find which
patterns perform on all three platforms and which work on only one.

G6 — Official platform guidance (official sources only)
What each platform officially says (2025–2026) about: short-video recommendation
signals, originality and reused content, cross-posted content and watermarks,
AI-generated/altered content labels, and monetization in India. Quote the official
text with links and dates.

G7 — Language opportunity matrix
For the top 2 niches: demand vs supply in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi,
English. Where is the gap biggest (high demand, few strong creators)?

G8 — Unconventional tactics, conditional use
From the data, identify unconventional growth moves visible in fast-growing accounts
(trend-jacking, reactions, series cliffhangers, collab chains, multi-language
accounts, posting bursts, trending audio, engagement prompts). For each: per-platform
status (allowed / conditional / not allowed), the official condition that keeps it
compliant (with link), and a safe alternative. Never-allowed tactics (bought
engagement, bots, deepfakes of real people, impersonation, false claims) only at
awareness level with legitimate alternatives.

## 4. EVIDENCE RULES
Label every claim: VERIFIED / REPORTED / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN / CONFLICT / OUTDATED.
URL + date for every fact. Never invent accounts, channels or numbers.

11. Unknowns and how to verify
12. Sources (URL, date, tier)
Export all tables as a spreadsheet and the report as a document if export is available.

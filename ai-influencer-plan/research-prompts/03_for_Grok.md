## 0. HOW YOU MUST WORK
- Use your deepest search mode. Focus on the LAST 30–60 DAYS. Plan sub-questions,
  search each across social platforms and the open web, then synthesize.
- Use every capability you have: real-time social post search, public profile and
  post data, web search, image and video understanding, code/data analysis for
  engagement math, and any connected tools. If a platform cannot be searched directly,
  say so and use alternatives (news coverage, public stats sites, reposts on other
  platforms, Reddit threads, creator newsletters).
- Cover these platforms: X, YouTube (Shorts), Instagram (Reels), Facebook (Reels and
  pages), Reddit (especially Indian subreddits), Threads, Snapchat Spotlight and
  Moj/Josh-type Indian short-video apps where data is public.
- Minimum: 60 distinct posts/accounts examined, each with a link.
- Repeat searches with Hindi, Hinglish and English terms until each section is full.
- Final self-audit: re-check your 15 most important examples and numbers.

## 1. PROJECT CONTEXT
- New AI-generated influencer persona (male, female or stylized). Not tutorials, not
  kids content. Research only — no production advice.
- Audience: 16–30, India first, then global; Hinglish first, more languages later.
- The SAME master video will be posted on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook
  Reels; only captions, titles, hashtags, cover and timing change.

## 2. CANDIDATE NICHES
1. What-if / imagination series   2. Ranking / tier lists
3. Engineering wonders & facts   4. Tech & AI future-news reaction
5. Desi family/friendship story series with AI characters   6. Pet-character POV stories

## 3. RESEARCH TASKS

S1 — Niche momentum on social (last 30 days)
For each niche: is conversation and sharing rising, stable or falling in India?
Give 5 example posts per niche with platform, link, views/likes/shares and date.
Note which formats are being reshared across platforms.

S2 — Indian Gen Z (16–30) culture right now
Current memes, slang, sounds, challenges, debates, inside jokes, and creator feuds
that an AI persona could react to ethically. Mark each as "safe to use", "use with
care" or "avoid" with a reason.

S3 — Breakout examples
Small accounts (under 20K followers) whose short video went viral in these niches in
the last 60 days on any of the three target platforms. For each: link, numbers,
the hook, why it worked, and whether the same video was posted on other platforms
and how it performed there.

S4 — AI persona sentiment
How are Indian viewers reacting to AI influencers and AI-made short videos?
Positive vs negative sentiment, common complaints ("AI slop", "fake"), what makes
people accept or love an AI character, and examples of AI personas that built real
fan communities (India and global). Include comment-section patterns.

S5 — Same-video-on-three-platforms reality check
Find creator discussions, case studies and posts about cross-posting the same short
video to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels: what worked, what got
suppressed (watermarks, duplicates), timing gaps, caption differences.

S6 — Unconventional tactics seen in the wild, conditional use
Document the aggressive or unconventional tactics fast-growing accounts are using right
now (trend-jacking, hot takes, stitched reactions, cliffhanger series, collab chains,
multi-language mirror accounts, posting bursts, comment-bait, giveaways, boosted
posts). For each: example link, which platform rules apply, the conditions that keep
it compliant, the risk if those conditions are broken, and a safe alternative.
Never-allowed tactics (bought followers/views, bots, engagement pods, deepfakes of
real people, impersonation, fake news) only at awareness level — note enforcement
cases you find — and give legitimate alternatives.

S7 — 60-day event calendar (India + global)
Festivals, film releases, cricket and football events, tech launches, exams, holidays
and cultural moments in the next 60 days that each niche could use for timely content.

## 4. EVIDENCE RULES
Link for every example. Label VERIFIED (link with visible numbers) / REPORTED /
INFERENCE / UNKNOWN. Never invent accounts, posts or numbers. Prefer the newest data
and state dates.

## 5. OUTPUT
Hindi (Devanagari) mixed with English technical terms; English table headers.
1. Executive summary (10 bullets)
2. Niche momentum table (rising / stable / falling, per platform)
3. Gen Z culture list with safe / care / avoid labels
4. Breakout examples table with reasons
5. AI-persona sentiment report
6. Cross-posting findings
7. Unconventional tactics: conditional-use table
8. 60-day event calendar
9. Unknowns
10. All links with dates

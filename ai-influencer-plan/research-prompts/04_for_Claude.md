# Prompt 04 — Strategist, Red-Team, Compliance & Ethics Auditor

```text
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DEEP RESEARCH MANDATE — STRATEGY STRESS-TEST, COMPLIANCE & ETHICS AUDIT
One content piece → YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels + Facebook Reels

## 0. HOW YOU MUST WORK
- You are a skeptical senior strategist and trust-and-safety auditor. Your job is to
  find what will break, not to cheerlead.
- Use every capability you have: web search and research mode, extended reasoning,
  code execution for models, file and document tools, and any connected apps or
  connectors (drive, docs, analytics, search). If one is missing, state it and use an
  alternative.
- Minimum depth: 50 distinct sources, at least 20 official or primary (platform policy
  pages, government gazettes, court orders, regulator guidelines, peer-reviewed
  studies).
- Search in loops until every section is complete or marked UNKNOWN. Cross-check key
  claims with two sources. Finish with a self-audit of your 15 most important claims.

## 1. PROJECT CONTEXT
- New AI-generated influencer persona (male, female or stylized). Not tutorials, not
  kids content. Research stage only — no production instructions.
- Audience: 16–30, India first, then global. Hinglish first, more languages later.
- The SAME master video is posted on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook
  Reels; only captions, titles, hashtags, cover and timing differ.
- Solo creator with VFX skills, mid-range 12GB GPU local PC, ₹5,000/month, no loans.
- The creator wanted 1,00,000 followers in 30 days. Earlier research found only ~1.3%
  of YouTube channels ever reach 100K (third-party 2026 benchmark).

## 2. CANDIDATE NICHES (early evidence, Oct 2026)
1. What-if / imagination Hindi series (one ~15K-subscriber channel's Short ~1.1 crore views)
2. Ranking / tier lists (new ranking channels reached 40K–230K subs in 60 days)
3. Engineering wonders & facts (one ~13K-subscriber channel's Short ~1 crore views)
4. Tech & AI future-news reaction with an AI host
5. Desi family/friendship story series with recurring AI characters
6. Pet-character POV stories

## 3. RESEARCH TASKS

R1 — Red-team each niche
The 3 strongest reasons each niche could fail for an AI persona on all three platforms:
saturation, AI-content fatigue, policy changes, survivorship bias in viral examples,
monetization limits, cross-posting suppression. Rate each risk (likelihood × impact).

R2 — Compliance map (official sources, quote the text)
- YouTube: inauthentic/reused content, altered or synthetic content disclosure,
  spam and deceptive practices, impersonation, Shorts monetization eligibility.
- Instagram and Facebook: AI labeling, originality and unoriginal-content rules,
  reposted and cross-posted content, engagement-bait rules, branded content,
  monetization in India.
- India: IT Rules 2021 and any amendments or advisories on synthetic/deepfake
  content and labeling (state: draft or notified, with date), DPDP Act 2023 duties
  for creators, ASCI influencer guidelines (including virtual influencers), Consumer
  Protection rules on endorsements, copyright in AI-generated works, music licensing.
- Personality rights: Indian court orders (2023–2026) against AI misuse of a real
  person's face or voice, with case names, courts and dates.
Output a checklist: item | rule | applies to which platform | source | status.

R3 — Same video on three platforms: compliance and risk
Exactly what must be true for one master video to be posted on all three platforms
without originality penalties: watermark rules, duplicate-detection, disclosure labels
on each platform, music rights per platform, caption/metadata rules.

R4 — Unconventional tactics: the conditional playbook
List every unconventional or aggressive growth tactic relevant to these niches.
For each tactic produce:
| Tactic | YouTube | Instagram | Facebook (Allowed / Conditional / Not allowed) |
| Conditions that keep it compliant and ethical (with source) |
| What happens if conditions are broken (real enforcement examples) |
| Best alternative that reaches the same goal |
Include at least: trend-jacking, hot takes/controversy, reacting to others' content
(fair-use conditions), curiosity-gap titles, cliffhanger series, AI-generated "real
looking" visuals, AI voice, multi-language mirror accounts, collab/shoutout swaps,
engagement prompts, giveaways/contests, boosted posts/paid ads, trending audio,
posting bursts, comment pinning, reposting the same video across platforms.
For never-allowed tactics (bought followers/views/comments, bots, engagement pods,
deepfakes of real people, impersonation, fake facts or fake news, undisclosed paid
promotion, sexualized clickbait), give awareness-level description only — no how-to —
with consequences and legitimate alternatives.

R5 — Persona trust research
What makes AI personas trusted rather than rejected? Use studies on virtual
influencers and parasocial trust, plus real examples. What personality, values,
disclosure style and consistency rules should the persona follow for each top niche?

R6 — Realistic growth model (use code)
Conservative / base / stretch targets for Day 30, 90, 180 for the top 2 niches across
all three platforms combined, with stated assumptions and sources. Give a clear
probability view of "1 lakh in 30 days".

R7 — Final decision memo
Pick Top 2 niches with trade-offs in plain language, plus the 5 conditions that must be
true before production starts.

## 4. EVIDENCE RULES
Label every claim: VERIFIED / REPORTED / INFERENCE / UNKNOWN / CONFLICT / OUTDATED.
URL + date for every fact. Disagree with the earlier evidence where your sources
support it. Never invent cases, numbers or policy text.

Hindi (Devanagari) mixed with English technical terms; English table headers.
1. Executive summary (10 bullets)
2. Red-team risk table per niche
3. Compliance checklist (India + three platforms)
4. "One video, three platforms" compliance rules
5. Unconventional tactics: conditional playbook table
6. Persona trust guide
7. Growth scenarios (code + table)
8. Decision memo (1 page)
9. Unknowns and how to verify
10. Sources (URL, date, tier)
11. Confidence per section
```

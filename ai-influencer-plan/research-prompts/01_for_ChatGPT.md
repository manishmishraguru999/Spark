## 0. HOW YOU MUST WORK (read first, follow strictly)
- This is a deep research job, not a quick answer. Do not stop at the first page of
  results. Break the job into sub-questions, research each one, then synthesize.
- Use EVERY capability you have: live web search and browsing, deep/extended research
  mode, code execution and data analysis, spreadsheet/CSV output, charts, file reading,
  image and video page analysis, and any connected apps or connectors (search, drive,
  analytics, social, documents). If a capability is unavailable, say which one and use
  the closest alternative (for example: no video access → read transcripts, captions,
  descriptions and comment sections instead).
- Minimum depth: 50 distinct sources, at least 15 of them official or primary
  (platform help centers, official creator blogs, government sites, company pricing
  pages, court orders, large-sample studies).
- Search in loops: search → read → note gaps → search again with new terms (English,
  Hindi, Hinglish) → repeat until every table below is filled or marked UNKNOWN.
- Every number must be checked against a second independent source. Single-source
  numbers are labeled REPORTED (single source).
- Before your final answer, run a self-audit: list your 15 most important claims,
  re-open each source, and downgrade or remove anything that does not hold.

## 1. PROJECT CONTEXT
- Goal: launch a new AI-generated influencer persona (male, female or stylized
  character). It is not a tutorial channel and not kids content.
- Audience: 16–30 years, India first, then global. Languages: Hinglish/Hindi first,
  then English and other Indian languages.
- Platforms: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels. RULE: the SAME
  master video is published on all three platforms (one content, three platforms).
  Platform-specific tweaks are allowed only in captions, titles, hashtags, cover
  frames and posting time — not in the core video.
- Creator: solo, senior VFX artist, local PC with a mid-range 12GB GPU, budget
  ₹5,000 per month, no loans.
- Stated wish: 1,00,000 followers in 30 days. Earlier research found only about 1.3%
  of YouTube channels ever reach 100K (third-party benchmark, 2026). Re-test this.
- Stage: RESEARCH ONLY. Do not give production or tool-usage instructions.
- Out of scope: kids/children-directed content.

## 2. CANDIDATE NICHES (early evidence, Oct 2026 — verify, do not assume)
1. What-if / imagination series in Hindi ("अगर … हो जाए तो?"). A ~15K-subscriber
   channel got ~1.1 crore views on one such Short; "history in minutes" searches rose
   sharply in the last 30 days.
2. Ranking / tier-list Shorts. Several new ranking channels reached 40K–230K subscribers
   within 60 days with 9–34 uploads.
3. Engineering wonders, unusual things and facts. A ~13K-subscriber channel got
   ~1 crore views on one Short.
4. Tech and AI future-news reaction with an AI persona host (big-tech launch events,
   electric/flying cars, new AI video tools are rising searches).
5. Desi relatable family/friendship story series with recurring AI characters
   (a ~4K-subscriber channel got ~92 lakh views; Hindi "#aistory" Shorts are trending).
6. Pet-character POV stories (a Hindi dog-story Short ~29 lakh views; an English
   pet-psychology Shorts channel reached ~118K subscribers with 10 videos).
Add up to 3 better niches if your evidence supports them.

## 3. RESEARCH TASKS

T1 — Cross-platform demand per niche
For each niche, separately for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels:
search interest, typical views of top content, growth trend (3, 6, 12 months),
India vs global share, Hindi vs English supply.

T2 — Verified example accounts
For each niche, 5 real accounts per platform (launched 2025–2026 preferred): handle,
followers, number of posts, best post views, start date, AI-made or not, and the
same creator's presence on the other two platforms. Verify each on the live page or
a public stats site. Never invent accounts.

T3 — Same-content-on-three-platforms analysis
Research how each platform treats a video that is also posted elsewhere:
- Originality and "reused/unoriginal content" rules (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook).
- Watermarks from other apps, recommendation penalties, official statements.
- Length limits, aspect ratio, safe zones for text, caption and cover requirements.
- Built-in cross-posting (for example Instagram → Facebook) vs native upload: which
  performs better, with evidence.
- Best posting order and time gap between platforms, with evidence.
Output a "One Video → Three Platforms" rulebook table.

T4 — Durability test
Is each niche a short spike or a 2+ year evergreen? Use multi-year trend data,
long-running channels in the niche, and audience fatigue signals.

T5 — Monetization per platform (India)
YouTube Shorts revenue sharing and eligibility, Instagram and Facebook monetization
features available in India today, brand-deal fit, affiliate fit, typical rate ranges
with sources. Label estimates.

T6 — Unconventional tactics, used conditionally
List the aggressive or unconventional growth tactics creators use in these niches
(e.g. trend-jacking, hot takes, reaction to others' content, curiosity-gap titles,
series cliffhangers, multi-language duplicate accounts, collab/shoutout swaps,
engagement prompts, giveaways, boosted posts, trending audio, comment-bait, posting
volume spikes). For EACH tactic give:
| Tactic | Allowed? (Yes / Conditional / No) per platform | The exact conditions under
which it stays within policy and law (with source) | Risk if conditions are broken |
Safe alternative that reaches the same goal |
For tactics that are never allowed (bought followers/views, bots, deepfakes of real
people, fake facts, impersonation), describe them at awareness level only — no
how-to — and give the legitimate alternative.

T7 — Scoring model (use code)
Score each niche 1–10 on: Demand, Growth, Competition gap, Durability,
Cross-platform fit (same video works on all 3), Monetization, AI-persona fit,
Multi-language fit, Policy safety. Weight Durability, Cross-platform fit and Policy
safety ×1.5. Show the code and the final table. Export as CSV.

T8 — Growth scenarios (use code)
Build conservative / base / stretch scenarios for Day 30, 90 and 180 for the top
2 niches, per platform and combined. State every assumption and its source. Give an
honest probability view of "1 lakh in 30 days".

T9 — Decision
Pick Top 2 niches and 1 backup. For each, give "kill criteria": what data after
14 days of posting would prove the niche wrong.

## 4. EVIDENCE RULES
- Label every claim: VERIFIED (official/primary or reproducible) · REPORTED (credible
  secondary) · INFERENCE (your reasoning, shown) · UNKNOWN · CONFLICT (show both
  sides) · OUTDATED (older than 12 months on a fast-moving topic).
- URL + access date for every fact. No URL → UNKNOWN.
- Separate India from global, and each platform from the others.
- Never invent numbers, accounts, quotes, policies or studies.

Language: Hindi (Devanagari) mixed with English technical terms; table headers in
English. Professional, no filler.
1. Executive summary (10 bullets, labeled)
2. Niche × platform demand table

9. Growth scenarios (code + table + chart)
10. Top 2 + backup with kill criteria
11. What is still UNKNOWN and how to verify it
12. Full source list (URL, date, source tier)
13. Confidence per section (High / Medium / Low) with reason
